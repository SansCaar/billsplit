# Nudges are Notifications, not Activities

Every recorded change in a Group is an Activity, but a Nudge is not: it is sent as a Notification only and never appears in the Activity feed. A Nudge changes no Expense, Settlement or Member, so putting it in the feed would mix requests for money with the record of what changed. The app still keeps when each Nudge was sent, to limit a pair of Members to 3 Nudges in any 24 hours and to show both of them how many were sent today. The cost is that a Nudge the recipient dismisses can't be found again, but the debt itself stays on the Balances tab.
