# Leftover cents are not distributed to anyone

When an amount does not divide evenly (e.g. $10.00 ÷ 3), each Member gets the rounded-down share and the leftover cents stay on the Expense as an Unassigned Remainder, added to nobody's share. We rejected the usual "give the extra penny to someone" rule because it makes shares look arbitrary; the cost is that Member shares on an Expense may sum to a cent or two less than its total, which the Payer effectively absorbs.
